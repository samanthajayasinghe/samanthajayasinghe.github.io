I've finally turned this domain into something more than a placeholder. This is
the first post, so it's as good a place as any to say what the site is for.

I'm a Site Reliability Engineer, and most of what I learn comes from running
systems rather than reading about them. The plan here is simple: write down the
things I'd want to have found when I was searching for them — the small fixes,
the operational patterns, and the automation that actually paid off.

Rough topics I expect to cover:

- **Operating Kubernetes / OpenShift at scale** — the failure modes you only
  meet once you have a lot of clusters.
- **Automating toil** — taking a runbook and turning it into something that runs
  itself.
- **On-call and incident response** — triage, alerting that earns its keep, and
  learning from what broke.

Nothing fancy, just notes worth keeping. More soon.
